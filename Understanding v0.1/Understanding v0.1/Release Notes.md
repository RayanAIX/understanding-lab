# Understanding v0.1

**Understanding Lab**  
**First Public Theoretical Release**  
**October 2026**

---

## What this release contains

This release presents the first public version of the Understanding Lab's working theory of understanding.

It consists of:

1. Definition of Understanding
2. Properties of Understanding
3. Non-Examples of Understanding
4. Taxonomy of Understanding
5. Operational Indicators
6. Definition Diagram

Together, these documents form a single theoretical package rather than independent claims.

---

## Status

**Version:** v0.1  
**Status:** Working theory  
**Release type:** Public research release

This is not presented as a validated theory or established measurement system.

The framework is a provisional synthesis intended to generate testable hypotheses and guide empirical investigation.

---

## What this release does

The package attempts to clarify:

- what understanding might mean;
- what properties may characterize genuine understanding;
- what forms of performance should not automatically be interpreted as understanding;
- how understanding might be classified;
- and which observable indicators may provide evidence about understanding.

---

## What this release does not establish

This release does not establish that:

- the proposed definition is correct;
- the listed properties are necessary or sufficient for understanding;
- the taxonomy represents the true structure of understanding;
- the operational indicators reliably measure understanding;
- or the framework has been empirically validated.

These remain open research questions.

---

## Research status

The current framework is intentionally open to revision.

The next stage of the Understanding Lab is empirical investigation.

The **Same Mind Project** will investigate whether individuals who produce similar or correct answers can nevertheless exhibit substantially different patterns across candidate indicators of understanding.

The results may:

- support parts of the current framework;
- weaken particular claims;
- reveal redundant indicators;
- reveal missing dimensions;
- or require substantial revision of the theory.
