# Changelog

All notable public changes to the Understanding Lab are documented here.

The laboratory preserves previous versions rather than silently replacing earlier ideas. This allows the evolution of its theories, frameworks, experiments, and evidence to remain traceable.

---

## [Understanding v0.1] — 2026-10-17

**Status:** Provisional

### Released

- Definition of Understanding
- Properties of Understanding
- Non-Examples of Understanding
- Taxonomy of Understanding
- Operational Indicators
- Definition Diagram
- Release Notes

### Purpose

This is the first public theoretical release of the Understanding Lab.

It establishes the laboratory's current working position on its central question:

> **What does it mean to truly understand something?**

The release proposes a provisional definition, candidate properties, non-examples, a taxonomy, and observable indicators that can be investigated empirically.

### Important limitation

Understanding v0.1 is a theoretical starting point, not a validated measurement system.

The proposed properties and operational indicators remain open to empirical testing, criticism, revision, or rejection.

### Next research direction

The next major research program is the **Same Mind Project**.

It will investigate whether people who produce similar or correct responses can nevertheless exhibit different patterns across candidate indicators of understanding.

### Versioning principle

The laboratory does not treat consistency with previous versions as a goal.

When evidence warrants change, theories and frameworks should change.

Earlier versions will remain available so that the reasoning and evolution behind later versions can be examined.
